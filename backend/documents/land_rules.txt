URBAN LAND REGULATIONS REFERENCE FOR SIS OFFICERS (TAMIL NADU)

This is sample reference material for the SIS chatbot knowledge base. It
summarises the land rules an urban Sub Inspector Surveyor applies while
verifying ISD (0154), NISD (0153) and MERGE (0155) applications in the
TAMILNILAM urban workflow. Figures marked "sample" are illustrative and must be
checked against the current Government Order before field use.

=== LEGAL BASIS ===

- Tamil Nadu Patta Pass Book Act, 1983 and Rules 1985 — patta issue, mutation,
  and pass book updates.
- Tamil Nadu Survey and Boundaries Act, 1923 — survey demarcation, boundary
  marks, F-line, and appeal survey.
- Tamil Nadu Town Survey Land Register (TSLR) Manual — urban survey number
  structure, sub-division numbering, TSLR extract and sketch.
- Registration Act, 1908 as applied in Tamil Nadu — sale deed registration,
  Encumbrance Certificate, IGRS Form 6, auto-mutation.
- Tamil Nadu Urban Land (Ceiling and Regulation) provisions — ULC land
  sub-division verification (service code 0164).
- Relevant Government Orders (GO) issued by the Survey and Settlement
  Department, including GO-506 dated 09/12/2023 for settlement of
  government poramboke to private (service code 0183).
  (Note: in the workflow, "SD" is the Senior Draughtsman who prepares the
  sub-division sketch — not the Survey and Settlement Department.)

=== LAND TYPE / CLASSIFICATION CODES ===

The land type code on a parcel controls what verification the SIS must do.
Sample mapping used in the urban extracts:

- 1 — Residential (நடைமுறை குடியிருப்பு)
- 2 — Commercial / mixed use
- 3 — Industrial
- 4 — Agricultural (wet / dry) inside town limits
- 5 — Government poramboke (road, channel, tank, burial ground)
- 6 — Institutional (temple, church, mosque, trust, educational)
- 7 — Vacant / unclassified

Rules that follow:
- A sub-division that changes land type (e.g. agricultural to residential) needs
  reclassification BEFORE the ISD sketch is finalised.
- Poramboke (type 5) cannot be sub-divided or transferred to a private owner
  except through a settlement service code (0179 / 0183) with the sanctioning GO
  quoted.
- Institutional land (type 6) transfer needs the endorsement of the controlling
  authority (HR&CE, Wakf Board, Diocese, or trust) on record.

=== MINIMUM EXTENT AND SUB-DIVISION LIMITS (SAMPLE) ===

- Minimum measurable urban parcel: 1 sq.m; areas recorded to 2 decimal places
  in square metres. Extent may also be shown in ares (1 are = 100 sq.m).
- Sample minimum plot for a residential sub-division inside a corporation:
  300 sq.ft (~27.87 sq.m) frontage-permitting; local body building rules
  override where stricter.
- A sub-division that would leave any resulting parcel below the local minimum
  is refused; the applicant is asked to revise the partition.
- Maximum sub-division depth: 3 levels (145 -> 145/1 -> 145/1A -> 145/1A1).
  Beyond this the officer records a remark and refers to DIS.
- Sum of all sub-division extents MUST equal the parent survey extent within a
  tolerance of +/- 0.5%. Anything outside tolerance needs re-survey and a DIS
  variance note.

=== NATHAM AND HOUSE-SITE LAND ===

- Natham (habitation) land is recorded in the urban natham chitta with an
  owner name, relative name, relationship code and ownership share.
- Natham settlement is service code 0188; owner name correction in TSLR is 0165.
- A natham parcel with ownership_share blank or "1/1,1" is treated as sole
  ownership; multiple owner rows on one patta_number are joint owners and every
  joint owner must sign at the field visit.
- Door number changes (new_door_number / old_door_number) are handled under
  street and door number modification (service code 0162), not under ISD.

=== HOW THE SUBMISSION CHANNEL IS DECIDED ===

Every application carries a submission channel - CSC, citizen or sub_registrar.
It is read off two columns of urban_application_log, and nothing else:

- source_name is "-" (no operator account touched the file): the application
  came in unattended from the Sub-Registrar, where IGRS raised the mutation off
  the registered deed. Channel = sub_registrar.
- source_name holds an operator or VLE code (samples "tut_tct_t131_02",
  "TNEFATUT0540-01") AND camp_flag = "P": the file was keyed in at a special
  revenue camp, where the operator enters it for the citizen standing in front
  of them. The submission is the citizen's own. Channel = citizen.
- source_name holds an operator or VLE code with any other camp_flag (blank,
  "S", "U", "Y"): keyed in at a Common Service Centre counter.
  Channel = CSC.

The CAN number's length does NOT decide the channel. It names the counter that
issued the number: 15 digits (133 series, sample "133280122203291") from a
CSC counter, 12 digits (sample "202329380999") from the TN citizen portal.
A camp application is a citizen submission but was keyed at a counter, so it can
carry either length. A Sub-Registrar application carries a 12-digit CAN whose
value is the same as its IGRS Form 6 number.

Do not use source_code to answer a channel question. It is populated on every
row with values 0 / 00 / 1 / 2 / 3 and splits both ways against every channel
signal, so it carries no channel meaning.

=== SRO, THE CAN NUMBER, AND WHAT AN ABSENT IGRS NUMBER MEANS ===

IGRS stands for Inspector General of Registration and Stamps (in Tamil, the Registration and Stamps
department -- பதிவுகள் மற்றும் முத்திரைகள் துறை / பதிவுத்துறை இன்ஸ்பெக்டர் ஜெனரல்). It registers
property documents and collects stamp duty through the Sub-Registrar Offices. Its services include the
Encumbrance Certificate (EC), guideline values, document registration and its status, and stamp duty and
registration fees. IGRS is not the "Indian Registration Society" and is not a grievance
system.

SRO stands for Sub-Registrar Office - the registration office where a sale deed
is registered. Officers say "SRO", "Sub-Registrar" and "sub registrar" for the
same office. When a deed is registered there, the SRO's IGRS system raises the
patta mutation off the registered document and issues an IGRS Form 6 number.
An application that reaches the SIS desk that way carries that number and no
operator code, and its submission channel is sub_registrar.

CAN stands for Citizen Access Number: the unique identity number a citizen is
issued and quotes on an application. Every application carries one.

Which applications carry an IGRS Form 6 number:

- A Sub-Registrar (SRO) referral always carries one. It names the registered
  deed the mutation was raised from, and its value is the same as that
  application's CAN number.
- A CSC application never carries one.
- A citizen (revenue camp) application never carries one.

What it means when the IGRS Form 6 number is ABSENT:

Only the SRO's IGRS system ever issues one. So an application with no IGRS
Form 6 number did not come from the SRO. It was filed at a CSC
counter, or by the citizen at a revenue camp. For those two channels the empty
IGRS field is the rule and not a gap in the record - there is nothing missing,
nothing to chase up with the Sub-Registrar, and the application is not
incomplete because of it. Read the other way: an IGRS number present is
positive evidence the file came from the SRO.

The CAN number's length is NOT a channel signal. It names the counter that
issued the number: 15 digits (133 series) from a CSC counter,
12 digits from the TN citizen portal. A Sub-Registrar referral therefore
carries a 12-digit, portal-issued CAN even though no citizen keyed it in, so a
12-digit CAN does not mean "citizen" and a 15-digit CAN does not mean "CSC".
The channel is decided by source_name and camp_flag, as set out above.

=== SALE DEED, EC AND REGISTRATION LINKAGE ===

- The sale deed number in the application (registration_document_number, sample
  form "R/520/1/1188/2022") must match the Sub-Registrar's registered index and
  the registration_place (e.g. "Tuticorin Joint I", "Tuticorin Joint II").
- IGRS Form 6 number is a 12-digit reference (sample "202223957844") linking the
  registered document to the mutation request. It appears on
  urban_application_log, the NISD/ISD application-info rows and the IGRS owner
  row.
- Encumbrance Certificate must show a clear title for the preceding 13 years and
  be within 3 months of its issue date at the time of verification.
- igrs_auto_mutation_flag = Y means mutation is expected to post automatically
  after registration; the SIS still verifies extent and boundaries before the
  patta transfer order is generated.
- transaction_status on the transfer detail: "01" = transfer effected,
  "02/NN" = refused with reason code NN, blank = not yet decided. Only "01" and
  blank are non-refusals.

=== BOUNDARY MARKS AND F-LINE ===

- Boundary stones carry a number; a moved, missing or renumbered stone is
  recorded with a photograph and GPS reading and flagged to SD.
- Disputed urban boundaries are resolved by F-line demarcation (service code
  0178); an unsatisfied party may seek an F-line appeal survey (0185).
- GPS coordinates are mandatory for every corner recorded during an ISD field
  visit; the officer notes the datum (WGS-84) and horizontal accuracy.

=== ENCROACHMENT: REGULATORY TREATMENT ===

- Private-on-public encroachment (onto road, channel, tank) is reported to the
  local body; the application cannot be approved until the encroached extent is
  removed or regularised by the competent authority.
- Public-on-private and neighbour encroachment is recorded with measured extent
  and a sketch; the transfer may proceed only for the un-encroached extent
  unless the parties settle.
- An encroachment flag set at the field visit is visible to SD and blocks
  automatic approval; resolution typically extends the timeline by 15-30 days.

=== LITIGATION AND STAY ORDERS ===

- A court reference in the EC or revenue records (sample form "O.S. 145/2023",
  "W.P. 8890/2024") puts the application on "On Hold - Litigation".
- Record the court name, case number, case type, parties and whether a stay
  order is active. No SLA clock runs during a litigation hold.
- The application resumes only on stay vacation, a judgment in the applicant's
  favour, or a registered settlement between the parties; a fresh field visit
  may be required after clearance.

=== REVENUE ARREARS ===

- Property tax, water charge and non-tax dues linked to the survey number must
  be cleared before the patta transfer order is generated.
- The Zonal Level Tahsildar (ZDT) confirms arrears status during review before
  approving the patta transfer order; unpaid dues are a documented rejection
  reason.

=== WHAT THE SIS MUST NOT DO ===

- Do not change a scheduled field visit date without Tahsildar approval.
- Do not approve a sub-division whose extents do not sum to the parent survey.
- Do not transfer poramboke or institutional land without the sanctioning
  authority's order on record.
- Do not proceed past an active court stay.
- Do not accept a sale deed that is not traceable in the Sub-Registrar index.

=== SERVICE FEE AND SLA BY SERVICE CODE ===

The fee an applicant pays and the time limit the office works to depend on the
service code. These are the three codes the SIS handles:

- 0153 (NISD, Not Involving Sub-Division): government fee Rs 100.00, plus a
  CSC counter charge of Rs 60.00 when filed through a counter.
  SLA: 15-20 working days.
- 0154 (ISD, Involving Sub-Division): government fee Rs 400.00, plus the
  Rs 60.00 CSC counter charge. SLA: 30-35 working days. The higher
  fee reflects the mandatory field inspection and the sub-division sketch.
- 0155 (MERGE, merge of sub-divisions): no government fee (Rs 0.00); the
  Rs 60.00 CSC counter charge still applies. SLA: 15 working days.

The government fee is a challan payment; the CSC charge is the counter
operator's service charge and is not levied when the citizen files on the
TN portal directly or the file comes in as a Sub-Registrar referral. There is
no separate SIS inspection fee - the field visit is covered by the 0154
government fee.

=== OWNERSHIP RECORD: WHAT IS HELD PER OWNER ===

Ownership of a survey number / sub-division is held as one row per owner, so a
jointly held parcel simply has several owner rows. For each owner the register
keeps: name (English and Tamil), the father's / husband's name with the
relationship (s/o, w/o, d/o), gender, address, the last four digits of the
Aadhaar number, and the ownership share. Two or more owner rows on the same
survey number means the parcel is jointly owned; a single row means a sole
owner.

The register does NOT hold an owner's mobile number, ration card number,
voter ID (EPIC), PIN code, occupation code or door number - those columns are
blank in the source extracts and are not projected. A question about one of
them is answered as "not recorded", not treated as missing data.
