=== SIS URBAN JURISDICTION: TAMILNILAM SERVICES & DATA SCHEMA ===

1. SIS ROLE IN TAMILNILAM URBAN
Sub Inspector Surveyors (SIS) operate exclusively in Urban Jurisdictions (Municipalities & Corporations).
Their core duties:
- Physical Field Inspection & Cadastral Verification for Town Survey Numbers (TSLR)
- Verifying boundaries, sub-divisions, encroachments, and land types (Residential, Commercial, Agricultural)
- Preparing inspection reports and measurement sketches (Colormap / FMB) for ISD, Merge, and Demarcation applications
- Forwarding verified ISD / Merge applications to the Senior Draughtsman (SD) for the sketch, then to the Deputy Inspector Surveyor (DIS); forwarding verified NISD applications directly to the Zonal Level Tahsildar (ZDT), who holds the DSC key and approves

2. URBAN APPLICATION NUMBER FORMAT
   YYYY / URBAN_SERVICE_CODE / DISTRICT_CODE / SEQUENCE
   Example: 2026/0154/02/000001 (ISD Application in Chennai)

3. URBAN SERVICE CODES HANDLED BY SIS
- 0153: Not Involving Subdivision (NISD) — Direct patta transfer verification (no new sub-division).
- 0154: Involving Subdivision (ISD) — Requires mandatory field inspection & sub-division sketch.
- 0155: Merge Subdivisions — Verification of merged plots & total area.
- 0156: TSLR Extract with Sketch — Urban land record extract with boundary sketch.
- 0157: TSLR Extract Only — Urban land record extract.
- 0158: Modification / Anadeenam — Correction/modification of urban survey records.
- 0159: Addition — Adding sub-divisions or owners in urban records.
- 0160: Deletion — Removing entries from urban records.
- 0161: Street Master — Street-level master record creation/update.
- 0162: Street and Door Number Modification — Modification of street and door number records.
- 0163: Block/Revoke Town Survey Number — Transaction freeze or revocation.
- 0164: ULC Land Subdivision — Urban Land Ceiling subdivision verification.
- 0165: TSLR Owner Name Correction — Rectification of owner name errors.
- 0167: TSLR Settlement - Owner Entry — Entry of owner details in TSLR settlement records.
- 0168: TSLR Settlement - Subdivision — Urban settlement subdivision.
- 0169: Govt to Private — Transfer of government land to private ownership.
- 0170: TSLR Sketch Verification — Verification of existing survey sketches.
- 0171: Rural-Urban Correlation — Correlation of rural survey numbers with urban records.
- 0172: Town Settlement — Settlement of town survey records.
- 0173: Temple Land Unblock — Unblocking of temple land records.
- 0175: Block Change — Change of block assignment in urban records.
- 0176: Govt to Govt Poramboke — Transfer between government poramboke accounts.
- 0178: F-Line (Urban Demarcation) — Demarcation of urban boundary lines.
- 0179: Settlement Govt to Private — Settlement transfer from government to private.
- 0181: Settlement Modification — Modification of existing settlement records.
- 0183: Settlement Govt Poramboke to Private (GO-506) — Special settlement transfer per GO-506 dated 09/12/2023.
- 0184: TSR Preparation Subdivision — Town Survey Register subdivision preparation.
- 0185: F-Line Appeal — Appeal survey for urban boundary demarcation.
- 0187: Register Patta — Registration of patta records.
- 0188: Natham Settlement — Settlement of Natham (habitation) land records.


4. ESSENTIAL FIELDS STORED FOR SIS
- Application Identity: application_id, service_code, application_type, application_date, application_status, workflow_state, last_updated_datetime, user_id (assigned SIS officer)
- Cadastral / Urban Location: district_code, taluk_code, urban_unit_code, ward_code, block_code, survey_number (Town Survey No.), subdivision_number, current_subdivision_number, patta_number
- Field Survey Data: field_visit_date, field_visit_scheduled, is_overdue, priority_flag, notes, sale_deed_number, sale_deed_registered, declared_reason, source_code, can_number
- Applicant Details: applicant_name, applicant_mobile, applicant_email, applicant_address
